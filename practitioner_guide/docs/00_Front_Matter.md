Sawubona.

In South Africa,where I come from,“sawubona”is the

Zulu word for “hello." There's a beautiful and powerful

intention behind the word because“sawubona” literally

translated, means, “I see you, and by seeing you, I bring

you intobeing."

At itscore,Emotional Agility is about the capacity to see

ourselves and others in a wholehearted and healthy way.

It's a set of essential psychological skills for our complex

world; a world that often chooses not to see. I am so

grateful to have the opportunity to share it with you.
